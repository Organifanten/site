Work conversations happen in work spaces. Moving to a restaurant or cafe changes the rules. The setting signals that this is not about deliverables. It is about each other. Those conversations stick.

## Theory

A change of scenery opens up new conversations. Avoid work topics. Talk about experiences, interests, or plans instead. This strengthens connection beyond the professional realm.

Building on the trust established in Activities 1–4 and the shared understanding developed through Level 2, you now take the relationship beyond professional boundaries. A shared meal is one of the oldest rituals for building connection because it combines physical comfort, unhurried time, and a setting that signals something other than work. The conversations that happen outside the office often carry more weight precisely because nobody is performing a role. This is where sensemaking — the team's shared interpretation of experiences — extends into personal territory.
