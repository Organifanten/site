Teams rush past their achievements. There is always another deadline, another problem, another thing to fix. This task forces a pause. Not to be complacent, but to acknowledge that we have done something worth acknowledging. The thank-you notes in Activity 15 were personal. This celebration is collective.

## Theory

Bring back the pride! List what you have accomplished, big and small. A moment of recognition boosts motivation and cohesion.

Kapitel 3 identifies rituals as social orientation points that give teams a sense of shared history, and celebration is one of the most powerful rituals available. When you name what you accomplished, you tell the team what matters, what worked, and what is worth repeating — the absence of that acknowledgment sends its own message. This exercise draws on the full arc of the program: you played games, listened deeply, wrote honest notes, mapped workflows, competed, and wished together. That is real work, and grounding the celebration in those specific experiences makes it authentic rather than performative.
