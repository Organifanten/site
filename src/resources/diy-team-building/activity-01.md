Walking side by side removes the pressure of eye contact and agenda. There is no table to hide behind and no slide deck to present. Movement creates a natural rhythm that makes silence feel comfortable instead of awkward. This is where people start to relax and say what they actually think.

## Theory

Movement and a change of environment help thoughts flow. Let conversation arise naturally — it's about getting to know each other, not about work. Use the time to understand how each person ticks.

A team is not a collection of roles but a living system of people who need to feel they belong before they can bring their full selves to work. Integration — the sense that we are part of something together — must come before differentiation, where individual perspectives and styles can surface without feeling threatening. This walk creates that foundation of connection through the simplest means available: time, presence, and a shared environment.
