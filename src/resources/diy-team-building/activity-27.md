Information that lives only in one person's head creates dependency and anxiety. An information radiator makes the invisible visible — progress, blockers, priorities, moods. The workflow clarity you built in Activities 17-20 gives you something worth radiating. Now you make it all accessible.

## Theory

A transparent overview of the team's status. Use a whiteboard or digital tool. Regular updates create focus and accountability.

A team is a socio-technical system, and the structures you put in place either support or undermine social behavior. An information radiator is one such technical structure: it makes progress, blockers, and priorities visible without anyone having to ask. When information lives in shared space rather than in individual heads, shared understanding grows and anxiety drops. This transparency also creates a natural accountability loop — what is visible becomes something the team collectively owns, which strengthens the identity work you started in Activities 25 and 26.
