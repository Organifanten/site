A team photo sounds simple, but it does something no meeting or email can. It freezes a moment and says: we exist as a unit. The trust you built in Levels 1-4 makes this possible — you need genuine comfort with each other before a shared image feels meaningful rather than forced.

## Theory

A photo with meaning and a wink. Choose the motif, location, and mood together. It can be fun. The main thing is that it reflects your team feeling.

A team is not just a collection of roles and processes — it is a social system that needs shared symbols to feel real. Language constructs team reality, and a shared image is one of the most immediate forms of that construction. When you choose together what the photo should look like, you practice sensemaking: aligning individual perspectives into a collective narrative. Place the result somewhere visible, like an information radiator, so it serves as a persistent reference point for who you are as a group.
