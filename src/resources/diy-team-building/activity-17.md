After four levels of building trust and connection, the team is ready for something more delicate: acknowledging that we are all a bit odd, and that the oddness is useful. Quirks and Edges Bingo turns the awkward process of admitting differences into a game, which makes it safer.

## Theory

A playful way to make individual traits visible. Keep it humorous, not hurtful. Afterwards discuss: which differences help us, which challenge us?

Kapitel 2 warns that without trust, differences feel threatening — and Kapitel 4 shows that self-knowledge and open communication turn those same differences into assets. The bingo format lowers the barrier to disclosure by wrapping it in humor, making it safe to name the habits others find baffling. Now that you have built trust through Activities 1–12, you can be honest about the edges that make you different without fearing rejection. Once surfaced, those quirks shift from silent sources of friction to openly acknowledged features of how the team works.
