At the end of a long journey together, words sometimes reach their limit. A symbolic gift asks you to translate what you have seen in a teammate into an image, an object, or a gesture. It is simple, a little vulnerable, and surprisingly powerful. The act of making something for someone, even something rough and handmade, communicates care in a way that a typed message cannot.

## Theory

Symbolic gifts work because they bypass the efficiency mindset. You are not optimizing. You are paying attention. The appreciation practices from Activities 9 and 10 taught you to notice what others contribute. The empathy work from Activities 14 and 15 taught you to feel into another person's experience. The identity exercises from Activities 25 and 26 gave you a language for who each person is beyond their role. This drawing exercise synthesizes all of that into a single act. It does not matter if the drawing is good. What matters is that it is specific, that it was made for one person, and that it carries meaning the team built together over many levels. From the first moments of getting to know each other in Activities 5 through 8, you have been gathering the material for exactly this kind of expression.
