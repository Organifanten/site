# Frequently Asked Questions

## 1. The Method

**What is Organifanten?**
I help people work on organizational tensions over time. The core idea is that most change work adds new things—frameworks, processes, behaviors. Organifanten does the opposite: it removes the hidden constraints that make current behavior the rational choice.

**What is Brain Budget?**
Brains are wired to conserve energy. That shapes how people behave in organizations more than most frameworks acknowledge. Understanding this changes how you design interventions.

**What is Via Negativa?**
Remove obstacles before you add solutions. Subtract before you add.

**What is Humble Consulting?**
The next step emerges through structured questions, not prescribed answers. The client discovers their own way forward, which means they actually own it.

## 3. Change Audit

**What is a Change Audit?**
A 1-week diagnostic: 10 interviews, materials review, 3-page findings document, and a 90-minute debrief. It surfaces how a specific change is actually landing in your organization.

**When should I use it?**
When you have a specific change underway and need to understand the hidden obstacles.

**How much does it cost?**
€5,000 total.

## 4. Culture Portrait

**What is a Culture Portrait?**
A 1-week characterization of your organizational culture. I spend time observing and talking to people, then deliver a 3-page portrait and a debrief. No recommendations unless you ask.

**When should I use it?**
Before you design any transformation or change. It gives you a baseline.

**How much does it cost?**
€5,000 total.

**How is this different from a Change Audit?**
A Culture Portrait looks at your overall culture now. A Change Audit looks at how a specific change is landing. Different tools for different moments.
