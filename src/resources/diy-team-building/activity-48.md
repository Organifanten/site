You made it. Forty-eight activities, twelve levels, and a collection of shared experiences that did not exist before you started. This final lesson is not a summary. It is a moment to stand in what you built together and let it settle. The celebration you planned in Activity 41 is the container. This reflection is the content.

## Theory

Everything in this game was designed to build on itself. The trust from Activities 1 through 4 made play possible in Activities 5 through 8. The appreciation from Activities 9 and 10 made identity work possible in Activities 25 through 28. The listening from Activity 13 made conflict engagement possible in Levels 7 through 9. The values work from Activities 27 and 28 made the charter revisions in Level 11 honest rather than performative. Nothing in this sequence was arbitrary. Each level earned the next. And now you are here, with a team that knows itself in a way it did not twelve levels ago. That knowledge does not disappear because the game ends. From the first walk in Activity 1 to this moment, you have been practicing something that matters: the slow, deliberate work of becoming a team that can trust, disagree, create, and celebrate together. The game is over. The practice continues.
